Coffee catering for Ottawa events

Ottawa hosts want more than an urn of coffee in the corner. Our mobile espresso bar arrives fully set up, with a barista pulling espresso and whisking matcha to order, so your guests get the same drink they'd line up for at their favourite café.

We work with offices, store openings and product launches as well as weddings and private celebrations. Every booking is built around your event: your branding, your guest count and your venue. A recent example is the H&M store opening in Ottawa, where the team's strawberry matchas were a highlight for the client.
