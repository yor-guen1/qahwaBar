About us

A coffee shop, wherever you're hosting.

Qahwa Bar started with a simple idea: bring the ritual of a proper coffee shop (the espresso, the care, the conversation) directly to the events people are already hosting. Since 2024, we've set up our mobile bar at weddings, corporate launches, and private celebrations across Ottawa and Montreal, pouring specialty espresso and matcha for guests who remember it long after the event ends. Every booking is built around your event: your branding, your guest count, your vibe.
