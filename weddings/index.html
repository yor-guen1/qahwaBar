Why couples book a wedding coffee cart

A coffee cart gives your guests something to enjoy between the ceremony and the dance floor: a flat white, a matcha latte, a proper espresso. It works at a cocktail hour, a dessert reception or a late-night send-off.

We plan the menu with you and handle the setup and service, so you don't have to think about it on the day. One couple told us it was "truly the best decision we made", and that everyone loved the coffee and matcha.
