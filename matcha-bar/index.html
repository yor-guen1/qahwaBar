A matcha bar for your guests

Not everyone wants coffee. A matcha option means the guests who skip espresso still get a drink made for them, and it gives your bar something people talk about. Our matcha is prepared to order at the cart, next to the espresso menu.

At the H&M store opening in Ottawa, the client singled out our strawberry matchas in their thank-you note. Ask us about flavours and milk options when you request a quote, and we will build the menu around your event.
