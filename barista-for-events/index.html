What you get when you hire an event barista

You are not just booking a person. We bring the cart, the equipment, the cups and the menu, and the barista runs the service, so you don't have to organize coffee for your guests on the day.

Every booking is built around your event: your guest count, your branding and your venue. Tell us the date and location and we will send a quote within 24 hours.

  • ✓A professional barista serving your guests
